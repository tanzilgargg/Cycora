# Cycora
## Circular Design Lab Browser Game

### Overview
Cycora is a browser-playable educational game about circular economy transformation. The core idea is that food service scraps can become textile resources through care, process, design, and reuse.

The game keeps the warm, kid-friendly Cycora style and simple typing controls, but the challenge now comes from memory, sequencing, matching, process decisions, timing pressure, product fit, and cause-and-effect.

### Controls
- Type the large word that matches your chosen action.
- Use `Backspace` to fix typing.
- In Practice Mode, the timer is removed.
- Mistakes give a hint and let the player retry.

### Six Levels
1. **Restaurant / Cafe**  
   Players greet tables, read patience, remember dishes and special requests, then see the specific scraps created by cafe service: citrus peels, banana fibers, avocado pits, coffee grounds, and more.

2. **Recipe / Lunch Rush**  
   Players move recipes through pantry, fridge, sink, chop, stove, and plate stations. Each recipe creates a useful scrap: citrus salad creates peels, corn soup creates husks, and coffee service creates grounds.

3. **Waste to Material Lab**  
   This replaces generic sorting. Players scan scraps, reveal properties such as pigment, fiber strength, texture, moisture, and dye potential, then match each scrap to its best transformation path.

4. **Fiber & Fabric Workshop**  
   Players move materials through process chains such as collect -> wash -> extract -> dye -> dry or collect -> peel -> extract -> spin -> weave. Quality changes with correct order and machine settings.

5. **Product Studio**  
   Players choose products that fit the fabric: strong woven cloth becomes a tote, soft dyed cloth becomes a scarf, and printed fabric becomes a pouch. Layout and finishing choices reduce waste and improve usefulness.

6. **Full Cycora Shift**  
   The final level combines the full journey: greet customer -> take order -> prepare recipe -> collect scrap -> discover material potential -> transform into textile -> create product -> send to reuse or community.

### Scoring And Rewards
The result screen shows stars, a sticker, scraps collected, material discoveries, textiles created, decisions made, mistakes, meals served, waste avoided, products made, people helped, and quality/recovery/fairness scores.

Level 6 also shows the full circular journey:
food served -> scraps collected -> material discovered -> textile created -> product made -> Eco Maker -> community support.

### Eco Maker Achievement Moment
When a level is completed, the finished product or achievement item is presented to the Eco Maker. Eco Maker then helps share it with a community member who can use it, such as a low-income family, shelter visitor, student, newcomer family, senior, food bank visitor, or community kitchen helper.

The result screen shows the product, source material, Eco Maker, community recipient, a short celebration message, and badges such as Waste Transformer, Product Maker, Dye Designer, Community Helper, or Circular Champion.

The achievement is saved locally in the browser. Each saved achievement includes:
- product name
- source material
- transformation path
- quality stars
- waste avoided
- reuse or community impact
- play session date label
- circular economy score

### Achievement Board
The Achievement Board is a friendly progress wall, not a stressful competition. It can be opened from the level map, gameplay HUD, or result screen.

The board displays:
- rank in the local collection
- completed level
- product created
- source material
- quality stars
- waste avoided
- reuse or community impact
- total circular economy score
- earned badges

Scores combine service, recipe progress, material discovery, process quality, product suitability, waste avoided, community impact, and mistakes.
